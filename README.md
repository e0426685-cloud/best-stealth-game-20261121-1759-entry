**SHADOW VAULT** — a complete top-down stealth game that plays offline in a desktop browser from its own files. You are the night infiltrator: slip through three guarded floors — the Gallery, the Office Floor, and the Vault — lift every intel file, and reach the exit unseen.

Guards patrol waypoints with sweeping vision cones and A* pathfinding; walls and crates block their sight, shadow patches hide you, and running is loud enough to draw an investigation. Get spotted and the detection meter fills: suspicious guards come to look, a full meter raises the alarm and they chase. Security cameras sweep the later floors. Collect all the intel to open the exit, then vanish — with a score for time, alarms, and clean getaways.

- 3 hand-built levels, each with its own patrols, cameras, cover, and shadows
- Sneak (Shift), walk, or sprint (Space) — speed trades against noise
- Guard AI: patrol → suspicious → investigate → chase, with line-of-sight checks
- Click-to-move plus WASD / arrow keys, synthesized WebAudio cues, best-score saving
- Deterministic screenshot scenes (`shots.json`) staged by playing the real rules

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome and press Enter to begin.
